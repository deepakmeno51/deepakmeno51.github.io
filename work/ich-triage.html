Resource-Efficient 3D Sequential AI Pipeline for Acute Intracranial Hemorrhage Subtype Triage and Rule-Out

A head CT pipeline that reads a scan as an ordered sequence of slices, flags acute intracranial hemorrhage and its subtype, and supports triage and rule-out decisions while staying within a small compute budget.

Problem

Emergency radiology queues need fast answers on head CT. A system that rules out hemorrhage reliably lets clinicians prioritize the scans that need attention first. Full 3D models cost memory and training time, so this project tests how far a lighter slice-then-sequence design goes.

System architecture

[Head CT series] --> [Preprocess: windowing, skull strip, slice alignment]
                            |
                  [Slice-level CNN encoder]
                            |
                  [Sequence model across slices]
                            |
        [Study-level output: ICH present / subtype / rule-out]
                            |
                   [Triage decision + Grad-CAM]

Pipeline

  1. Take head CT series from the RSNA Intracranial Hemorrhage Detection dataset
  2. Apply brain windowing, skull stripping and slice alignment
  3. Encode each slice with a CNN backbone (ResNet18 and DenseNet-121 compared)
  4. Model slice order with a sequence network and attention pooling
  5. Predict hemorrhage and subtype at the study level
  6. Map scores to a triage decision, with a rule-out threshold
  7. Check what the model looked at with Grad-CAM
